  1. Animal-origin-free medium enhances G2/M progression while maintaining identity and genomic stability in human adipose-derived MSCs
    Stem Cell Research & Therapy · 2026-10-05 · DOI 10.1186/s13287-026-05149-4

    Across three adipose MSC donors and early to late passages, an animal-origin-free medium gave the shortest doubling time versus serum and serum-free media, enriched G2/M cell-cycle programs, and kept ISCT identity markers and copy number stability.

    msc adipose_msc xeno_free media manufacturing genomic_stability
  2. Off-the-shelf cryopreserved adipose tissue and ADSCs accelerate epithelial regeneration
    Tissue & Cell · 2026-10-06 · DOI 10.1016/j.tice.2026.104017

    Adipose-derived stem cells stored for two months at -80 C closed rat wounds faster than cryopreserved adipose tissue or controls by day 3 (about 65% vs 54% vs 43%), with similar trends in ex vivo human skin.

    msc adipose_msc cryopreservation off_the_shelf wound_healing
  3. Identification and Functional Characterization of CDK1, CCNB1, CCNB2, RRM2, and GTSE1 Associated with Replicative Senescence of Human Umbilical Cord-Derived Mesenchymal Stem Cells
    Current Stem Cell Research & Therapy · 2026-10-09 · DOI 10.2174/011574888X508076260925100358

    Five cell-cycle genes (CDK1, CCNB1, CCNB2, RRM2, GTSE1) rose with passage in UC-MSCs across four donors alongside classic senescence features at passage 12, though the authors say causality is not established.

    msc umbilical_cord_msc replicative_senescence passage_limits biomarkers
  4. Synergistic reversal of mesenchymal stem cell senescence by engineered exosomal delivery of a dual miR-195/miR-188 sponge
    Experimental Gerontology · 2026-10-08 · DOI 10.1016/j.exger.2026.113352

    Wharton's jelly MSC exosomes loaded with a miR-195/miR-188 sponge lowered p16, p53, IL-6, ROS and beta-gal in replicatively senescent adipose MSCs and were linked to restored telomere length and clonogenic capacity.

    msc wharton_jelly_msc exosomes engineered_evs senescence mirna
  5. Bioreactor cultures of brain endothelial cells produce extracellular vesicles with comparable characteristics as that of batch cultures
    bioRxiv (preprint) · 2026-10-06 preprint · DOI 10.64898/2026.10.05.756840

    A CELLine AD1000 bioreactor kept brain endothelial cells about 90% viable for up to eight weeks and gave three to four times more EV protein than flasks, with comparable particle properties, mitochondrial cargo and ATP rescue.

    evs bioreactor scale_up manufacturing mitochondrial_transfer
  6. TriPort tangential flow filtration: a lab-assembled system for EV isolation across diverse cell-culture sources
    bioRxiv (preprint) · 2026-10-06 preprint · DOI 10.64898/2026.10.03.756465

    A lab-built, lower-cost tangential flow filtration rig reproducibly isolated EVs from three culture sources, and adding size-exclusion chromatography mattered most for serum-containing media, where TFF alone co-concentrated serum proteins.

    evs tff isolation manufacturing conditioned_media
  7. Cytokine priming shifts mitochondrial architecture and extracellular vesicle cargo of feline placental mesenchymal stromal cells toward an immunosuppressive phenotype
    American Journal of Veterinary Research · 2026-10-05 · DOI 10.2460/ajvr.26.05.0197

    In placental MSCs from seven cats, priming with TNF-alpha, IFN-gamma, IL-1 and IL-6 kept viability above 96%, elongated mitochondria and shifted EV cargo toward anti-inflammatory miRNAs such as miR-204 and let-7g.

    msc perinatal_msc licensing evs mirna veterinary
  8. Mesenchymal stem cell-derived extracellular vesicles modulate Th1- and STAT1-associated inflammatory responses in peripheral blood mononuclear cells from patients with osteoarthritis
    Molecular Biology Reports · 2026-10-07 · DOI 10.1007/s11033-026-12850-3

    MSC EVs (20 ug per million PBMCs, 48 h) cut Th1 cell frequency in PBMCs from 10 osteoarthritis patients from about 34% to 27% and lowered STAT1, IL-1 beta, IL-6 and TNF-alpha.

    msc evs immunomodulation osteoarthritis potency_assay
  9. Integrated evaluation of umbilical cord mesenchymal stromal cell-derived extracellular vesicles across skin-related cellular and zebrafish models
    Frontiers in Bioengineering and Biotechnology · 2026-10-09 · DOI 10.3389/fbioe.2026.1939569

    One defined UC-MSC EV preparation reduced oxidative injury in keratinocytes, lowered UVB-induced fibroblast senescence and raised zebrafish fin regeneration from about 60% to 84% at 48 hours.

    msc umbilical_cord_msc evs skin photoaging zebrafish
  10. Phase IIa Trial of Locoregional High-Dose Autologous Natural Killer Cell Therapy Combined with Hepatic Arterial Infusion Chemotherapy for Locally Advanced Hepatocellular Carcinoma
    Cancer Research and Treatment · 2026-10-08 · DOI 10.4143/crt.2026.0477

    In 16 evaluable patients with refractory locally advanced liver cancer, hepatic arterial autologous NK infusion after HAIC gave an independently assessed response rate of about 69% and median overall survival of 24 months, with no severe NK-related toxicity.

    nk autologous_nk clinical_trial hepatocellular_carcinoma locoregional
  11. Fibrinogen depletion prevents non-dissociable cell aggregates of feeder-derived NK cell products
    Frontiers in Immunology · 2026-10-01 · DOI 10.3389/fimmu.2026.1887148

    Macroscopic aggregates in feeder-expanded NK products came from feeder cells plus plasma fibrinogen together, and swapping autologous plasma for a fibrinogen-free serum substitute prevented them without hurting expansion, viability or killing.

    nk manufacturing feeder_cells formulation aggregation
  12. Functional Exhaustion Limits CAR-NK Therapy in Glioblastoma but Can Be Mitigated by Early Repeated Dosing
    Research Square (preprint) · 2026-10-04 preprint · DOI 10.21203/rs.3.rs-10808000/v1

    B7H3 CAR-NK cells killed glioblastoma faster than CAR-T in vitro but lost function after repeated tumor challenge and failed in mice, while early repeated dosing, not TGF-beta or IL-15 engineering, improved survival.

    nk car_nk exhaustion dosing glioblastoma
  13. PTPN22 as a novel therapeutic target: a key intracellular checkpoint for NK cell therapy
    Journal for ImmunoTherapy of Cancer · 2026-10-01 · DOI 10.1136/jitc-2026-016896

    Deleting or inhibiting PTPN22 boosted NK and CAR-NK killing and cytokine release, preserved function under TME-like cytokine stress, and prolonged survival in a leukemia xenograft.

    nk car_nk gene_editing checkpoint persistence
  14. Targeting HLA-E-NKG2A/CD94 to reverse pyroptosis-induced immunosuppression in HCC enhances NK cell antitumor activity
    Research Square (preprint) · 2026-10-06 preprint · DOI 10.21203/rs.3.rs-11115596/v1

    NK killing caused tumor pyroptosis and IL-10 release that pushed macrophages to an HLA-E-high M2 state, which suppressed NK cells through NKG2A, and blocking that axis restored NK activity in vitro and in mice.

    nk nkg2a hla_e macrophage hepatocellular_carcinoma
  15. Decline of chaperone-mediated autophagy in aging impairs macrophage clearance of senescent cells
    Nature Aging · 2026-10-06 · DOI 10.1038/s43587-026-01240-w

    Age-related loss of chaperone-mediated autophagy made senescent cells more aged-like and their SASP blocked macrophage clearance, while drug activation of CMA cut senescent cell burden in old mice and eased lung fibrosis.

    senescence immune_clearance macrophage autophagy sasp
  16. uPAR-Targeting T Cell Engager Exerts Senolytic Effects in Mice and Non-Human Primates With Serum Aminotransferase Activity as a Safety Monitor
    Aging Cell · 2026-10-01 · DOI 10.1111/acel.70742

    A uPAR-directed T cell engager cleared senescent cells and eased age-related pathology in aged mice and monkeys at low dose, while high doses caused liver toxicity that serum transaminases flagged.

    senolytic upar bispecific immunotherapy non_human_primate
  17. Methylation-associated Gene Expression (MaGE): a non-invasive, in vivo measure of cellular senescence
    medRxiv (preprint) · 2026-10-01 preprint · DOI 10.64898/2026.09.29.26364335

    Blood DNA methylation proxies for p16, p21 and p14ARF expression, tested in 18,859 people, showed 45 significant links to 18 incident diseases and mortality and tracked treatment response in Crohn's disease.

    senescence biomarker dna_methylation p16 p21 trial_endpoint
  18. Chronic Senolytic Treatment and/or Aerobic Exercise Reduce Senescence and Improve Myocardial Function During Aging in the Heart
    Aging Cell · 2026-10-07 · DOI 10.1111/acel.70759

    Nine months of dasatinib plus quercetin, aerobic exercise, or both in middle-aged mice lowered p16+, p21+ and gamma-H2AX+ heart cells and improved diastolic function, with no clear added benefit from combining them.

    senolytic dasatinib_quercetin exercise cardiac_aging
